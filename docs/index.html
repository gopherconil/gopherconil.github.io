2026 conference cancelled

🇮🇱GopherCon Israel 2026

Israel's Go community conference

We really tried. But with heavy hearts, we've decided to cancel this year's GopherCon Israel. Tech conferences worldwide are struggling, and we're no exception. We'll wait for better times and return with the conference you deserve. Shana Tova!

Originally planned for October 18 at Expo - Bitan 10, Tel Aviv.

About

GopherCon Israel 2026 will not take place.

GopherCon is focused on the Go programming language, and the Israeli community around it is still going strong. While we won't meet at a conference this year, we'll keep sharing news, talks and meetups through our community channels.

Thank you to everyone who submitted talks, sponsored and supported this year's plans. We'll wait for better times and return with the conference you deserve.
